Buying a phone under ₹15,000 is about picking the right trade-offs. Here is a simple checklist.

## 1. Processor and RAM
Look for a recent 5G chipset and **at least 6 GB RAM** if you keep many apps open. 8 GB is better if you play games.

## 2. Storage
Choose **128 GB or more**. Check whether the phone has a memory card slot.

## 3. Display
A 90 Hz or 120 Hz screen feels smoother. AMOLED gives better colours and outdoor brightness than LCD.

## 4. Battery and charging
5,000 mAh is standard. Check the charging speed **and** whether the charger comes in the box.

## 5. Software updates
Check how many years of Android and security updates the brand promises. It decides how long the phone stays safe to use.

## 6. Before you buy
- Compare the same model on Amazon, Flipkart and Croma. Prices differ, and bank offers change often.
- Read recent user reviews for heating and battery complaints.
- Check the return and replacement policy.
